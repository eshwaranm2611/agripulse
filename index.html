why this matters

Farming runs on guesswork it shouldn't have to

Most smallholder farms and storage points still rely on manual checks and instinct — by the time a moisture drop or a cold-chain failure is noticed, the damage is already done. India's agritech sector is scaling fast, but sensor-driven decision-making still reaches only a small fraction of farms. AgriPulse is built to close that gap with hardware that's cheap enough to deploy at scale.

the loop

How AgriPulse works

01 — sense

Cheap sensors, everywhere it matters

Low-cost soil, weather, and cold-storage sensors report moisture, temperature, humidity, and tank levels every few minutes over low-power connectivity.

02 — understand

One dashboard, plain language

Readings are turned into clear status — good, watch, act now — instead of raw numbers a farmer has to interpret themselves.

03 — act

Alerts before damage happens

SMS and app alerts fire the moment a reading crosses a safe threshold — a dry field, a warming cold store, a pest-risk spike — with enough lead time to actually respond.

the platform

Built for farms and the supply chain after them

01

Field monitoring

Live soil moisture, temperature, and pest-risk tracking per plot, with historical trends to plan irrigation and sowing.

02

Cold-chain tracking

Temperature and humidity monitoring across storage and transit, catching spoilage risk before produce is lost in transit.

03

FPO & co-op dashboards

One platform, many farmer groups — each co-op sees only its own members, plots, and produce, with shared market pricing data.
